INTRODUCTION

Have you heard the story of Darth saket the wise? Its not a story that they would tell you

SwerveDrives are one of the most complex and difficult mechanisms in FTC, in an area where improved performance is often underapreciated. It can be difficult, frustrating, confusing and often far more time consuming than other drivetrains. Especially in the presence of Mecanum drives, which dominate due to their sheer simplicity and reliablity, as well as their continued viability in ever season, unlike swerves which can be useful in one season and completely useless in another it is almost never advised.

Swerve is a pathway to many abilities some consider to be ... unnatural...

On the other hand, when they do work, Swervedrives can be absolutely broken. Kneebreaking maneuvers, increased traction, insane speed, extreme defensive and counter defensive properties all come with the package of a working swervedrive. They can enable absurd teleops and push the ceiling of autos.

do you think you have what it takes?

That said, SwerveDrives are far from perfect. They can have diffculties driving in straigt line, and often doing something known as the "swerve wobble". They come with a myriad of mechanical and software issues not encountered elsewhere and often take a lot of obscure and esoteric knowledge to get working. Learning all of it via trial and error can wear down ones resolve, and eat into the precious little time within a season.

So you want to build a SwerveDrive?

If you're still going to do this, you better do it right. Swerve Manual 0 has all of the required knowledge in one place. First, make sure that you have at least 4-5 of following:
enough budget
dedication
experience
at least 1 good CADer
at least 1 good programmer
and a solid plan and understanding of swervedrives If the last three are what you still need, you've come to the right place.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

Make sure that swerve is even viable in the current season. For a comeptition that is movement heavy, such as centerstage or DECODE, a swervedrive might be the deciding factor in a match. However, in seasons where the ideal robot eliminates movement entirely, such as Into The Deep, swervedrives are almsot useless. while the aura is undeniable, its not nearly as worth it if the game isnt movement based.
