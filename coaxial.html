COAXIAL

INTRODUCTION

Coaxial swerve, is a very straightforward way to do a drivetrain. For movement, it is simply powered by 1 motor per pod, with a single servo to power Azimuth rotation. due to using a coaxial shaft, it suffers from the effects of coaxial rotation, otherwise known as the twin "coax effect"

Pros:
"simpler hardware"
"easier to program"
4 wheels, which enables complete drivetrain and more traction
fits a bit better into parallel plate packaging
more modular
complete and tested autos with multiple pathers
more popular
cons:
more current draw
takes up 4 servo slots
weaker azimuth, and thus possible issues with "swerve wobble"
The "coax effect"

Encoders
Using an External encoder is both better and cheaper than using the internal axon encoder, as a swyft servo on continuous rotation is around as powerful as an axon. That said, many teams utilize the axon's built in encoder due to convenience as the axon mini is a very powerful servo.
